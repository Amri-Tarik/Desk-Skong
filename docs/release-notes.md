**Windows 10/11 · needs your own copy of Hollow Knight.** The art is read from your
install the first time you run it; nothing from the game is included.

**Install:** download `Desk-Skong-…-windows-x64.zip` below, unzip it anywhere (it's
portable, no admin rights) and run **`skong.exe`**. A small window finds your Hollow Knight
install and reads the art (a few seconds), then the knight appears. See the README for the
details.

### What he does

- **Explores your desktop** like Hallownest: runs along window title bars and lines on
  screen, wall-jumps, dashes, crystal-dashes, dives, and sits on benches he finds.
- **Finds things in your apps:** benches, Geo rocks to mine, shinies, and grubs trapped in
  jars waiting to be set free (not every jar holds a grub).
- **Has visitors:** Cornifer, Quirrel, Myla, Zote, Elderbug, Hornet and Sly drop by to talk,
  map, sing, spar, race or teach him a thing or two.
- **Fights:** crawlids and friends wander in, the odd gauntlet, and a duel with your cursor
  if you insist. If he falls, his Shade keeps his Geo until he wins it back.
- **Pastimes** when it's quiet: a training dummy, chasing lumaflies, taking in the view,
  stargazing at night.
- **Secrets:** 43 hidden achievements, and something waiting once enough are found.
- **Stays out of your way:** never moves your cursor or windows, steps off a monitor when a
  fullscreen game starts, hides from screen shares by default. No network connections.

The control panel (opens with him) holds the journal and the settings; the tray icon has
the same switches.

### Reporting a bug

Turn on *Write a log file* (tray or panel), make it happen again, then attach the newest
`skong-….log` (and any `crash-….log` / `trace-….log`) from `%APPDATA%\Desk-Skong\logs` to an
issue. Have a look first: logs mention window and program names.
